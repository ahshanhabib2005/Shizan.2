About Me

My background, technical skills, projects, and future vision

1. Introduction & Future Aspirations

Background & Education

I am currently pursuing a Diploma in Electrical Engineering at Western Ideal Institute (WII) in Dhaka. Since childhood, I have had an intense curiosity about how electronic circuits, motors, and devices function under the hood. Beyond academic theory, I thrive in hands-on practical lab work, circuit assembly, prototyping, and coding.

Future Plan: Japan Journey

My immediate goal after completing my diploma is to move to Japan—the global epicenter of robotics, mechatronics, and precision hardware engineering. I aim to work in high-tech laboratories and industries there to absorb their work discipline and advanced technologies.

Long-Term Vision (Tech Startup)

Building upon the knowledge and expertise gained from Japan, my long-term vision is to establish my own technology and hardware startup. I aspire to develop cost-effective automation and robotics solutions to address complex challenges in industrial automation, agriculture, and smart living.

2. Technical Skills & Tools Analysis

I don't just code or build circuits; I leverage an entire technical ecosystem for problem-solving:

Hardware & Microcontrollers

  • ESP32 & ESP8266: Used for IoT remote data monitoring and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth wireless control systems.
  • Arduino (Nano/Uno): My go-to tool for rapid circuit logic testing and basic sensor integration.
  • Raspberry Pi: Applied for complex processing, OS-based applications, and intermediate robotics.
  • Sensors & Modules: Ultrasonic, MQ Gas, Soil Moisture, LDR, PIR, Flame Sensor, and SIM800L GSM Module for real-time environment data processing.

Programming & Web Tech

  • Embedded C++ & Python: C++ for real-time logic execution on microcontrollers and Python for data handling or Pi projects.
  • Web Technologies: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for building custom IoT web dashboards.
  • IDE Tools: Arduino IDE and VS Code for writing, debugging, and configuring libraries.

Visual Design & Media Tools

  • Adobe Illustrator: Designing circuit diagrams, vector artwork, logos, and t-shirt graphics.
  • Photoshop, Lightroom & Snapseed: Post-production color grading and photo processing.
  • CapCut: Video demonstration editing for technical projects and short promotional media.
